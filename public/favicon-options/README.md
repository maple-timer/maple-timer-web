# Favicon Options

These are original Maple Timer favicon candidates. They do not reuse official Nexon or MapleStory game assets.

1. `01-quickslot-digit.svg` - quickslot cooldown digit, product-specific.
2. `02-maple-clock.svg` - refined maple leaf and clock.
3. `03-cooldown-ring.svg` - timer ring with cooldown digit.
4. `04-slot-grid.svg` - quickslot grid with one active slot.
5. `05-bell-timer.svg` - notification bell and timer.
6. `06-mt-mark.svg` - neutral Maple Timer monogram.
7. `07-yellow-countdown.svg` - bold yellow cooldown number.
8. `08-mushroom-clock-face.svg` - orange mushroom silhouette with clock face.
9. `09-mushroom-stopwatch.svg` - stopwatch body under mushroom cap.
10. `10-mushroom-cooldown-number.svg` - mushroom and cooldown number badge.
11. `11-mushroom-badge-clock.svg` - compact mushroom with small clock badge.
12. `12-minimal-mushroom-clock.svg` - simplest mushroom and clock hand.
13. `13-pixel-mushroom-timer.svg` - pixel-style mushroom and cooldown number.
14. `14-mushroom-alarm-clock.svg` - mushroom alarm clock silhouette.
15. `15-leaf-timer-classic.svg` - bold generic maple leaf and timer.
16. `16-leaf-cooldown-number.svg` - maple leaf with yellow cooldown number.
17. `17-leaf-bell-alert.svg` - maple leaf and alert bell.
18. `18-leaf-scan-frame.svg` - maple leaf inside crop scan corners.
19. `19-leaf-stopwatch.svg` - maple leaf wrapped around stopwatch.
20. `20-mob-silhouette-clock.svg` - orange mob-like silhouette with clock face.
21. `21-mob-cooldown-badge.svg` - orange mob-like silhouette with cooldown badge.
22. `22-npc-silhouette-timer.svg` - NPC-like silhouette with timer badge.
23. `23-pixel-mob-cooldown.svg` - pixel-style mob silhouette with cooldown number.
24. `24-mob-alarm-silhouette.svg` - orange mob-like alarm clock silhouette.

Open `preview.html` to compare them at favicon sizes.
